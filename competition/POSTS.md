## Skool — Promote Your Work

Title: SayPay — Ask understands. You organizes.

SayPay is a Productivity Mini App for people who invoice, track time, and get paid in NIM.

Flow: type or upload in Ask → Gemini files it → you confirm anything that moves money or starts a chat → You keeps the record.

- Ask: one input. Tasks, reminders, receipts, invoices. Money always shows a confirm card, then Nimiq Pay signs.
- Work: jobs, timer → invoice, numbered invoices (SAY-2026-NNN), optional two-party escrow. SayPay AI is only the tiebreaker. Votes never move NIM.
- Chat: search a SayPay ID, they accept or decline. Encrypted. Ask cannot read it.
- You: editable records + an activity log of things that actually changed.

NIM only. No simulated balance. Open it in Nimiq Pay.

Demo: https://saypay-payment-assistant.emerxch.workers.dev
Repo: https://github.com/emer-eth/saypay

Testers: claim a SayPay ID, invoice a friend, try “remind me Friday to follow up”, upload a receipt photo.
